# Changelog

What each version of Parliament changed, in plain words, newest first. When a
new version is waiting, the update notice shows the first lines of its entry.

## 0.22.0, 17 November 2026

- The bar stands a little above the page, open or folded into a circle, with a soft shadow and no dark ring when you type in it. Its grey words go when you tap or click into it, and an empty search reads "Search here".
- On a phone the bar rises above the keyboard, the pills read "framework" and who answers by name alone, and the microphone sits at the end of the command bar. Highlighted words bring the keyboard up to change them, and the bar stays open while you do, so a prompt spoken there goes in the text on them.
- A room's page draws no outline or tint on your own text, and "Write here or type a prompt in the bar." goes when you click into it.
- Frameworks shape how the model responds, and you can add one of your own in their menu: a name, what it asks, and Add.
- The ink pot drains under the pointer and fills again when it leaves.

## 0.21.0, 17 November 2026

- On a phone, the bar is a plus, your words, a microphone and the ink pot, which sends. Tap into a prompt and the frameworks and who answers show as pills under your words. Swipe the bar left or right for search, command and prompt, and a label above it says which.
- The plus holds the boards, Import: and Tour:, Markup, Focus, the language and your alarms, so the boards have the whole screen. They stand one above another, the width of the phone: scroll between them, or use the arrows at the right edge, and the bar follows the board you see.
- Voice in every mode, on a computer too, from the microphone beside the glyph. Speech is turned into words on your device, and nothing goes before Enter. A prompt spoken with words selected in a reply goes in the text on them. Where a browser would send your audio away, the microphone is faint, and a click says why.
- A room's kind, such as Writing Studio, waits for the Lab: a room shows its number, its title, and a name you gave it with name:. The Records table leaves it out too.

## 0.20.0, 16 November 2026

- Alarms: alarm:25m tea, alarm:15:30 "Call Anna", or alarm:every 50m "Take a break" for a break after so much work. When one rings, a band says so, with stop and 5 more minutes, and the strip counts down to the next. The Alarms board lists them, and alarm:quiet turns the chime off.
- A dictionary: dictionary:reluctant, or Ctrl+Alt+L (⌘⌥L on a Mac) on a word in your text, shows what it means and the words that mean the same, and a click puts one in its place as an edit. thesaurus: does the same. The word lists come with Parliament, in English for now, and nothing is sent.
- Sums: calc:48000*2/12 works one out under the bar, and figures selected in your text show their sum and average.
- Tab and Shift+Tab move a line in and out, up to three steps, and a list's item becomes a sub-item. Ctrl+Shift+E, R and L (⌘⇧E, R and L on a Mac) put lines at the centre, the right and the left, and align: and indent: do the same from the bar. Escape, then Tab, leaves the text.
- Comments and highlights stay on their words as you change them, and go only when all their letters do.
- The Collection: its boxes show, its legend reads removed, added and model answer, its "date" order is "newest first", and a comment brings it beside the room.
- The "/" menu's checkbox ticks in a room, and with the page full, a new board opens and the page scrolls to it.

## 0.19.0, 16 November 2026

- Tasks: the Tasks board lists every task, each with a box to tick. Add one with the plus, or new:task "…" in the bar. A task has a name, a description if you like, a kind (yours, a prompt, a search or a command), a model, rooms and a flow: one by one, or all at once.
- A task's sub-tasks run with run: and the task's number. A to-do of yours holds the ones after it until you tick it, and a command waits in the bar for your Enter. Each sub-task can go into its task's room, a room of its own, or a branch of the task.
- What were lists are tasks now, and their tasks are sub-tasks: new:subtask 207 "…", tick:207 and tick:207 2. The older words still work.
- Write in an empty room to start one. Your text stays a draft, changed without marks, until a model reads it; with Suggesting on, your changes to a draft are marked. Each branch keeps its own copy.
- The strip writes Import: and Tour: as it writes the boards, the birds fold the boards away and back, and Focus works on an empty room.
- Boards fill the page down and across before they get narrower.

## 0.18.0, 16 November 2026

- Parliament opens on an empty room, with the bar at its foot. Closing the welcome page and reset: leave an empty room too, and tour: brings the welcome steps back.
- The strip starts with the birds, and has import: and tour: after Plan:.
- Notes are comments now. Select words in a reply and press Ctrl+Alt+M (⌘⌥M on a Mac) to comment on them, or write comment:reply for the whole reply. Comments show in the text and are never sent.
- Asks are prompts in the text now. Select words and press Ctrl+Alt+P (⌘⌥P on a Mac): the words sit between brackets with your prompt after them, and the answer comes back to accept or reject, or in the reply itself if you choose "as a reply".
- Above the bar, a list shows what Enter sends to each branch: your words, your prompts in the text and your edits, each to that branch's own model. Untick one to hold it back. With nothing typed, Enter sends just what waits.
- Your first mark in a chat brings the Collection up beside the room. Its order is a Sort by menu next to its tabs, and its name and sources sit on one line.

## 0.17.0, 15 November 2026

- markup ▾ at the end of the strip, where calm was, opens one menu for what shows in the text: edits, highlights and prompts, each shown or hidden. With edits hidden, a reply reads as it stands and you can still type in it.
- In the same menu, Suggesting keeps what you type held back, drawn dashed, until you accept it, and Editing makes it the text. Accept all and Reject all settle what waits in the room, your held edits included.
- calm: and the markup switches on rooms and the Collection are folded into the menu. The Collection always shows its marks.
- The bar stays where it is when the welcome page closes or focus turns on or off. Only bar: moves it.
- Turning focus off keeps the boards you opened while focused, and a room alone on the screen is as wide as in focus.
- Your first edit or ask starts a collection, as a first highlight does, and the Collection no longer counts its cards.
- Parliament's own building blocks are taken only once they are a week old, and none of them runs scripts as it installs.

## 0.16.0, 6 November 2026

- Tick an edit, an ask or a suggestion in the Collection, and what can be done to it (hold back, undo, accept, reject) appears at the foot of the board.
- In calm, the Collection shows its cards and their edits in full: nothing opens as the pointer passes.
- The menu of who answers stays open until you click outside it, and its order is always shown.
- A summary, a comparison by meaning and a chat's summary are written in your language when the model cannot tell what the text is in.

## 0.15.0, 5 November 2026

- In calm, a reply reads as it stands, even while you edit it, and no longer moves as the pointer passes over it. The room's markup switch shows the edits.
- The Library waits for the Lab, in a later release: it leaves the strip, and library: says so. Nothing in it is deleted.

## 0.14.1, 5 November 2026

- reload on the notice of a new version works in every window, where it sometimes did nothing.
- install: no longer misses the browser's offer when the page is slow to start, and says so when Parliament is installed as an app on this computer already.

## 0.14.0, 5 November 2026

- reset: clears the screen to the start card: every board and window closes. Nothing is deleted.
- The start card fits its words and sits in the middle of the screen, with the bar at the foot.
- focus on the strip is one word in the margin, and a click turns it on or off.
- Setting up a model on your own computer is five numbered steps, with the command for your system.
- The language selector no longer covers the welcome page, and the installed app's icon has rounded corners.

## 0.13.0, 5 November 2026

- ? opens with the everyday commands. more shows the rest, and ?all shows every command at once.
- A click that changes a setting says what it ran under the bar, and undo: takes it back.
- Continue on a cut-off reply writes continue: in the bar, which names the model that wrote the reply and the cost before you press Enter.
- The network log in Settings says what each request was for, and keeps it until you close the tab.
- Less said on screen: Escape is a row of ?, the menus lose their how-to-click sentences, and Settings' switches say on or off.
- A room says when edits wait for your next prompt, and a highlight says it is kept.

## 0.12.0, 4 November 2026

- compare:201a 201b sets the last replies of two branches side by side and marks the words that differ. Nothing is sent to an AI model.
- compare: compares words, not meaning, and says so. Add meaning at the end, compare:201a 201b meaning, to ask a model how they differ in meaning: one call, its model and cost named before you press Enter.
- When two texts share few words, compare: says the marks tell you little, and offers to ask about meaning.
- Japanese and Chinese are compared word by word, split by your browser's own dictionary.
- The welcome page's advanced steps open from Advanced, right under the steps, and number on from them.

## 0.11.0, 2 November 2026

- Any key you add answers: a chat with no model of its own goes to the first provider you have a key for, and to Wikipedia only with no key at all.
- new:chat opens another room beside what is here, and room:"Owl notes" opens a chat by its name.
- One message by command: quote:reply, copy:reply, delete:reply, note:reply and tagadd:favorite reply act on the message you are in. A favourite is now the tag favorite.
- The Collection takes filters, export:md and pin:"name", as Records does.
- open: with a note's, highlight's or quote's number opens it where it is. inspect:highlights lists a room's highlights, and pop:settings keys opens Settings in a window at its keys.
- tour:advanced is the welcome page's second list. letter: is a board you can move, and the command menu shows lines of several commands.
- install: installs Parliament as an app where the browser offers it, with everything you have here.
- A line of a Wikipedia answer that names a page it links to can be asked about next. A popped board shows the birds until it is ready, and a second Enter no longer sends a prompt twice.

## 0.10.0, 2 November 2026

- spread: open:202 203 205 collection records spread opens them all across your screens, in equal groups in the order named. spread: alone spreads what is open again.
- open: takes several at once, and arrange:save "Morning" can end a line, keeping what it made.
- A line of several commands says what each one will do.
- A highlight is marked only where you selected it. Keywords are now highlights; tags label and search finds.
- The language sits in the header, and the footer links to the source.

## 0.9.0, 31 October 2026

- Several boards can share one window, so each screen can be one window: pop:records 202 collection display 2. beside puts a board in the window of another: pop:library beside records.
- move:library display 3 moves a board to another screen. A window sent to a screen fills it, and has a full screen mark in its corner.
- Closing a window closes its boards; nothing in them is lost.
- The languages open right under the globe, and with the bar at the bottom, api: and the other panels open above it.
- The welcome page names the room by its number, and a first highlight starts a collection with its chat.

## 0.8.0, 30 October 2026

- Parliament speaks Chinese, Japanese, Swedish and Finnish, in beta. Pick one with the globe or language:. Spanish is coming soon.
- close: works on every board, close:room among them. Closing the welcome page leaves the bar in the middle of the screen, with how to open a room.
- In the menu of who answers, a click on a name switches to it for the rest of the chat; its box adds it beside the others.
- An edited prompt waits in the bar as "Your edited prompt" instead of filling it, and calm marks an edited reply with a quiet line, not a tick.
- The tab shows the three birds, a room shows markup · clean once it holds edits, and the welcome page's step 9 needs no key.
- Answers from Wikipedia are only ever read, and the link to an article tells Wikipedia the visit came from Parliament.

## 0.7.0, 29 October 2026

- One bar commands every board. A board popped into a window of its own has none, and opens at the size it had on the page.
- A room is its chat, in a calmer type, and a selection can run from a prompt into its reply.
- The menus for who answers, frameworks and languages are laid out like the list of commands, and a click outside closes them.
- The list of commands can stay open as a board: open:commandmenu, or pop:commandmenu for a window of its own.
- The ink pot shows even while a quick answer arrives.

## 0.6.0, 29 October 2026

- A room beside another board keeps its side columns folded, so the chat has the room's width. A button at each side opens one over the chat.
- ? lists every command, in columns across the screen.
- settings: opens Settings, and who answers is no longer hidden under the quill.
- This release is built around the default room. The Lab and the other rooms come in a later release, and any room you made still opens.
- The welcome page is its steps and the bar, and letter: opens a letter from the one who made Parliament.

## 0.5.1, 28 October 2026

- The hashes published with the release now match the files the key vault serves, so anyone can check it. Those of 0.5.0 were made from a copy with Windows line ends.

## 0.5.0, 28 October 2026

- A new Parliament: everything on screen is a board, and one bar does the searching, the commands and the prompts.
- Everything you have made is in Records, by number. The first visit upgrades what you had, and keeps a copy first.
- Edit any reply in place, ask for changes, and send your edits with your next prompt.
- Ask several models at once, as branches, or plan a run of prompts on the Plan board.
- Your keys move into Parliament's key vault, where code on the page cannot read them.
- Rooms are built in the Lab, from blocks, layouts and themes.
- Ask Wikipedia, Hacker News or OpenAlex with no key, or a model on your own computer.
- Lock Parliament, saved arrangements, calm, focus sets, other screens, and a layout for small screens.
