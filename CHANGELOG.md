# Changelog

What each version of Parliament changed, in plain words, newest first. When a
new version is waiting, the update notice shows the first lines of its entry.

## 0.12.0, 4 November 2026

- compare:201a 201b sets the last replies of two branches side by side and marks the words that differ. Nothing is sent to an AI model.
- compare: compares words, not meaning, and says so. Add meaning at the end, compare:201a 201b meaning, to ask a model how they differ in meaning: one call, its model and cost named before you press Enter.
- When two texts share few words, compare: says the marks tell you little, and offers to ask about meaning.
- Japanese and Chinese are compared word by word, split by your browser's own dictionary.
- The welcome page's advanced steps open from Advanced, right under the steps, and number on from them.

## 0.11.0, 2 November 2026

- Any key you add answers: a chat with no model of its own goes to the first provider you have a key for, and to Wikipedia only with no key at all.
- new:chat opens another room beside what is here, and room:"Owl notes" opens a chat by its name.
- One message by command: quote:reply, copy:reply, delete:reply, note:reply and tagadd:favorite reply act on the message you are in. A favourite is now the tag favorite.
- The Collection takes filters, export:md and pin:"name", as Records does.
- open: with a note's, highlight's or quote's number opens it where it is. inspect:highlights lists a room's highlights, and pop:settings keys opens Settings in a window at its keys.
- tour:advanced is the welcome page's second list. letter: is a board you can move, and the command menu shows lines of several commands.
- install: installs Parliament as an app where the browser offers it, with everything you have here.
- A line of a Wikipedia answer that names a page it links to can be asked about next. A popped board shows the birds until it is ready, and a second Enter no longer sends a prompt twice.

## 0.10.0, 2 November 2026

- spread: open:202 203 205 collection records spread opens them all across your screens, in equal groups in the order named. spread: alone spreads what is open again.
- open: takes several at once, and arrange:save "Morning" can end a line, keeping what it made.
- A line of several commands says what each one will do.
- A highlight is marked only where you selected it. Keywords are now highlights; tags label and search finds.
- The language sits in the header, and the footer links to the source.

## 0.9.0, 31 October 2026

- Several boards can share one window, so each screen can be one window: pop:records 202 collection display 2. beside puts a board in the window of another: pop:library beside records.
- move:library display 3 moves a board to another screen. A window sent to a screen fills it, and has a full screen mark in its corner.
- Closing a window closes its boards; nothing in them is lost.
- The languages open right under the globe, and with the bar at the bottom, api: and the other panels open above it.
- The welcome page names the room by its number, and a first highlight starts a collection with its chat.

## 0.8.0, 30 October 2026

- Parliament speaks Chinese, Japanese, Swedish and Finnish, in beta. Pick one with the globe or language:. Spanish is coming soon.
- close: works on every board, close:room among them. Closing the welcome page leaves the bar in the middle of the screen, with how to open a room.
- In the menu of who answers, a click on a name switches to it for the rest of the chat; its box adds it beside the others.
- An edited prompt waits in the bar as "Your edited prompt" instead of filling it, and calm marks an edited reply with a quiet line, not a tick.
- The tab shows the three birds, a room shows markup · clean once it holds edits, and the welcome page's step 9 needs no key.
- Answers from Wikipedia are only ever read, and the link to an article tells Wikipedia the visit came from Parliament.

## 0.7.0, 29 October 2026

- One bar commands every board. A board popped into a window of its own has none, and opens at the size it had on the page.
- A room is its chat, in a calmer type, and a selection can run from a prompt into its reply.
- The menus for who answers, frameworks and languages are laid out like the list of commands, and a click outside closes them.
- The list of commands can stay open as a board: open:commandmenu, or pop:commandmenu for a window of its own.
- The ink pot shows even while a quick answer arrives.

## 0.6.0, 29 October 2026

- A room beside another board keeps its side columns folded, so the chat has the room's width. A button at each side opens one over the chat.
- ? lists every command, in columns across the screen.
- settings: opens Settings, and who answers is no longer hidden under the quill.
- This release is built around the default room. The Lab and the other rooms come in a later release, and any room you made still opens.
- The welcome page is its steps and the bar, and letter: opens a letter from the one who made Parliament.

## 0.5.1, 28 October 2026

- The hashes published with the release now match the files the key vault serves, so anyone can check it. Those of 0.5.0 were made from a copy with Windows line ends.

## 0.5.0, 28 October 2026

- A new Parliament: everything on screen is a board, and one bar does the searching, the commands and the prompts.
- Everything you have made is in Records, by number. The first visit upgrades what you had, and keeps a copy first.
- Edit any reply in place, ask for changes, and send your edits with your next prompt.
- Ask several models at once, as branches, or plan a run of prompts on the Plan board.
- Your keys move into Parliament's key vault, where code on the page cannot read them.
- Rooms are built in the Lab, from blocks, layouts and themes.
- Ask Wikipedia, Hacker News or OpenAlex with no key, or a model on your own computer.
- Lock Parliament, saved arrangements, calm, focus sets, other screens, and a layout for small screens.
