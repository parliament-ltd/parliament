# Changelog

What each version of Parliament changed, in plain words, newest first. When a
new version is waiting, the update notice shows the first lines of its entry.

## 0.7.0, 29 October 2026

- One bar commands every board. A board popped into a window of its own has none, and opens at the size it had on the page.
- A room is its chat, in a calmer type, and a selection can run from a prompt into its reply.
- The menus for who answers, frameworks and languages are laid out like the list of commands, and a click outside closes them.
- The list of commands can stay open as a board: open:commandmenu, or pop:commandmenu for a window of its own.
- The ink pot shows even while a quick answer arrives.

## 0.6.0, 29 October 2026

- A room beside another board keeps its side columns folded, so the chat has the room's width. A button at each side opens one over the chat.
- ? lists every command, in columns across the screen.
- settings: opens Settings, and who answers is no longer hidden under the quill.
- This release is built around the default room. The Lab and the other rooms come in a later release, and any room you made still opens.
- The welcome page is its steps and the bar, and letter: opens a letter from the one who made Parliament.

## 0.5.1, 28 October 2026

- The hashes published with the release now match the files the key vault serves, so anyone can check it. Those of 0.5.0 were made from a copy with Windows line ends.

## 0.5.0, 28 October 2026

- A new Parliament: everything on screen is a board, and one bar does the searching, the commands and the prompts.
- Everything you have made is in Records, by number. The first visit upgrades what you had, and keeps a copy first.
- Edit any reply in place, ask for changes, and send your edits with your next prompt.
- Ask several models at once, as branches, or plan a run of prompts on the Plan board.
- Your keys move into Parliament's key vault, where code on the page cannot read them.
- Rooms are built in the Lab, from blocks, layouts and themes.
- Ask Wikipedia, Hacker News or OpenAlex with no key, or a model on your own computer.
- Lock Parliament, saved arrangements, calm, focus sets, other screens, and a layout for small screens.
