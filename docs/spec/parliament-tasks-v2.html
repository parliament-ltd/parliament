Tasks, rethought

C301 (g), redrawn after you tried the first build. What was a plan is a main task, numbered as before (207), with sub-tasks. Each has a title. A sub-task says who does it, a model or you, and goes to one or more branches, each with its own model and its own room, or no room at all. The runs and the counts sit under the main task.

Tasks need no room: the Tasks board lists every main task, and an answer shows under its sub-task. A room is chosen only where you want one. One small change to what is stored, in section 5.

5 · What is stored: shown before building

The main task keeps its record and title (a docket). A sub-task stays a step in each branch it goes to, as a prompt to several is now; the steps of one sub-task share an id, and gain a title. Who does it and each branch's room are stored already.

  • title: optional, up to 200 characters. A step without one shows its first words, as now.
  • group is the field a prompt to several already writes (C202): one sub-task across its branches. A step without one is a sub-task of its own.
  • A branch's room is the field each branch of a run has now (room, or null for none).
  • No migration. A restore checks title; backups carry it; older ones read as now; an older Parliament drops it and shows the words.

6 · To rule

  1. The names: a main task (task 207, new:task) and its sub-tasks (sub-task 2, new:subtask), in place of list and task; older words still read.
  2. The board lists every main task, one open under the list at a time.
  3. A sub-task is one row however many branches it goes to, a chip for each branch with its model and its room or none; a sub-task of yours has neither.
  4. Answers show under their sub-task, so a task needs no room; a room is chosen per branch, when wanted.
  5. Runs and counts are listed under the main task.
  6. The stored change: title on a sub-task's step.
