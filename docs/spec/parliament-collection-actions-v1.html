A · The actions of the cards you pick, at the foot

Cards show their facts only. Ticking a card adds its own actions to the row at the board's foot, beside Note, Tag, Summarize and Prompt, which already act on what is picked. Here edit 241 is picked.

→ Collection · Ran hold:241. undo: takes it back (after a click on Hold back)
  • A click on Hold back runs hold:241 at once and says so under the bar, as a card's action does now; undo: takes it back. Typed in the bar, it does the same without picking.
  • Several picked of one kind (two edits): their shared actions. Of mixed kinds: Note, Tag, Summarize and Prompt only.
  • An answered ask offers Accept and Undo; a suggestion Accept and Reject; a held edit Let it go and Undo.
  • The cards are quieter, and acting takes one click more: tick, then act. The foot row stays where it is, so the eye learns one place.

B · Each card's actions, always drawn

As C297 left it, in calm too: every card that can be acted on keeps its one or two actions in its marginalia, after a thin rule, in the margin's grey. Nothing waits for the pointer.
