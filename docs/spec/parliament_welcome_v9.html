    Now, let’s

    Parliament is a place to work with AI on your own terms. It is early, and still being built.

    It runs in your browser, with no servers and no accounts. Your chats and keys stay on this computer, and each request goes straight to the provider you pick, to a model running on your own machine, or just Wikipedia. There are no ads.

    With the goal of rethinking how we interact with AI, it is yours to shape. Rooms are built from blocks, layouts and themes. You create the space, you choose how to interact.

    Most AI apps are built for passive consumption. You ask something, you receive a response. Here you steer from a command line: branch chats, highlight, add and combine notes across chats, plan a run of prompts, and more. Let’s take back control.

    — So Lov
