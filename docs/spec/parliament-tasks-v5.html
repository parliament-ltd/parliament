Tasks, v5

v4 made simpler. A task is added by typing on its own row, beside an empty box, as a to-do list does. Click in and the grey words go; a Model pill appears at the end of the row. Press Enter and the task is yours, done. Pick the pill and the rest opens under the name: a description, the three kinds, who answers, rooms, the flow, and Save.

No labels on the left: each field says what it is by its grey words until you write in it. Nothing has a "change" link: a name, a description, a sub-task's words are written where they stand, and pills open their lists where they are. "None" and "Kind" are gone: with no kind on, the task is yours.

3 · A task open, written in place

The open task is laid out as the row that added it, so what you set is what you see. Click the name or the description and write; it is kept when you leave it or press Enter, and Escape puts it back. The pills open their lists where they are. The sub-task you are in opens its pills; the others rest.

5 · Other ways to make it quicker

You asked for more. Each can be taken or left on its own.

Enter keeps you typing

Enter saves the row and leaves the caret on a fresh one, so a list is typed as fast as you think it. On the open task, Enter in "Add a sub-task" does the same, each new sub-task starting as its task.

Paste a list, get sub-tasks

Paste five lines into "Add a sub-task" and it offers five sub-tasks, one per line, each yours and named by its line. Pasted text is never read as a command: a line starting with > is a name like any other.

5 lines pasted · Enter adds them as 5 sub-tasks, Escape keeps them as one name

A Run button

Drawn in section 3. A task with something to send shows Run in place of the small run:207 link. It puts run:207 in the bar, whose line says what goes and what it costs, and Enter runs it, as now: the board still never sends by itself.

Drag to reorder

One by one, the order is the order they run in. A grip on hover moves a sub-task up or down; Alt and the arrow keys do it from the keyboard. Nothing new is stored: sub-tasks are already kept in order.

Found while drawing: today a tick, a new task or a deleted sub-task is said under the bar and taken back by undo:, but a name, a description or a model changed on the board is kept with neither. Written in place, each change would be said and taken back the same way.

Runs on one line

Drawn in section 3. The latest run on one line under the task, with where it waits; earlier runs behind open:run 1. The "Runs" heading and its list go until there is more than one to show.

An empty board says nothing

With "Add a task" a field you can type in, "No tasks yet" and the line under it have nothing left to say. The board would show the row alone; new:task stays in the reference.

6 · What is stored

Nothing changes. The fields are v4's: a task's title, description, model, rooms, flow and doneAt; a sub-task's title, its words, model, command and into. A task with no kind on is one with no model and no command, as "None" was. Reordering moves sub-tasks within the order already kept. No migration; every backup reads as before.

7 · To rule

  1. The row that adds: an empty box and a field, "Add a task" gone when clicked; Enter saves a task of yours.
  2. The Model pill: shown while the caret is in the row; it opens the description, the kinds, who answers, rooms, the flow and Save, with Prompt on. It also leads to Search and Command, which have no model; "Model" as you named it, or another word such as "More"?
  3. No labels, no "change": grey words name each field; names, descriptions and words written where they stand, kept on leaving, Escape putting them back.
  4. Three kinds: Prompt, Search, Command; picked again to turn off; none on means yours. "None" and "Kind" go.
  5. The flow switch: A or B.
  6. Save, with Cancel kept faint beside it, since a phone has no Escape.
  7. Section 5: which of a to g to build with it.
