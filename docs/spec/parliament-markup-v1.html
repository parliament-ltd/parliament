  • One branch, so the bar stays one line and its line says it all. With nothing typed, Enter sends what waits: "Enter sends 3 prompts and an edit in 201a". Ctrl+Enter does the same from inside the text.
  • In Editing, your change is the text at once and the model is told of it with your next prompt, as edits are now. In Suggesting, it stays marked until you accept it. The model's answers come back as suggestions in either mode.
  • The prompt in the second paragraph asks a question; its row in the list can send it "as a reply" instead (section 2).

2 · The bar, when prompts wait in more than one branch

The list opens above the field by itself, because what waits spans two branches; otherwise a click on the line opens it. Each branch with something ticked gets a request of its own, with only its own prompts and edits, so each answer is that branch's and lands in it. An unticked item is held back, as in the Collection: it waits for the next send.

  • Your typed words go to each ticked branch, with that branch's own prompts. Untick 201b and they go to 201a alone.
  • It covers the room the bar is addressing. Prompts waiting in another room go when that room is addressed.
  • With several answerers ticked, each branch's request goes to each of them and every answer is a new branch, as now. The head then counts those requests and their cost.
  • The dashed row is for later: comments stay unsent in the first build, and the row comes only if testing shows they are missed.

3 · Writing a prompt or a comment in the text

Select words, then Ctrl+Alt+P for a prompt or Ctrl+Alt+M for a comment. A box opens at the passage, as the ask box does now. Enter keeps it; Escape lets it go.

4 · The colours, as each kind of colour blindness sees them

Simulated at full strength. About one man in twelve has some red and green colour blindness; blue and yellow blindness is rare. No colour is safe alone, so every mark also has its own shape: underlined, dotted, dashed, bracketed with p>, a tint with a bubble.

5 · To rule

  1. Vermillion for prompts, with the brackets and p> as its shape.
  2. The list: it opens by itself when what waits spans more than one branch, and otherwise on a click on the line.
  3. Answers in the text unless a prompt's row says "as a reply".
  4. Comments not sent in the first build; the dashed row only if they are missed.
  5. Mode and Show apart, in the one menu, Editing first.
  6. The order: this menu and the modes, then comments, then prompts in the text with the list, then the room to write in, then tasks.
