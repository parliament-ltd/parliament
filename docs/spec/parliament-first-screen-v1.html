First screen

C302 (d), to rule. The first screen is a room. On the first run, an empty room with the birds and three commands; after the tour is closed, or after reset:, an empty room alone, the bar centred at its foot. The welcome steps are a command away, tour:. Nothing new is stored: an empty room exists today, and its first prompt starts its chat. Writing your own text in it before any prompt is C301 (f), and waits for its own stored change.

1 · The first run: two ways to place the birds

Today the first run shows the welcome page with its steps. Instead, a room, addressed, and the bar under it resting on a prompt. The birds and their three lines either stand beside the room (A) or sit in the empty room itself until its first prompt (B).

4 · To rule

  1. Where the birds go on the first run: beside the room (A), or inside the empty room until its first prompt (B).
  2. Closing the tour and reset: an empty room alone, the bar centred under it, as section 2 draws.
  3. The start card retires, its three lines carried by the bar's line; the component kept in src/deprecated/ until you approve its removal.
