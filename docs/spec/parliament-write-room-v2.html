A room to write in

C301 (f), redrawn after your two rulings. The first screen is already an empty room (C302). Here it becomes a page you can type into: your own text, never sent by itself, marked like any reply, with prompts written in it and answered in it. A prompt typed in the bar still starts a chat as now.

Ruled since v1: branches keep their own copy of your text, and in a draft, Suggesting marks your changes. New here: section 3 (Suggesting in a draft) and section 6 (branches, and the moment they are made). Your text is one new kind of message, shown in full in section 7.

2 · Writing, and marking it

What you write is yours, and is never sent by itself. Everything a reply takes, it takes: headings and lists as you type them, a comment (Ctrl+Alt+M), a highlight, a prompt in the text (Ctrl+Alt+P), "as a reply" on a prompt's row. Until a model has read it, it is a draft: in Editing, what you type is simply your text, nothing marked and nothing numbered.

3 · Suggesting, in a draft

As ruled: with the markup menu on Suggesting, your changes to your own draft are marked, dashed, as a held edit is, numbered, and listed in the Collection. Accept one and it goes into your text, with nothing to tell, since no model has read it; reject it and it is taken back.

4 · The answer, in your text

Each prompt in the text comes back where it was asked, as the model's suggestion, to accept or reject. With nothing typed, the model is told to answer only in those places, so a reply that holds nothing else is one line under your text, not a card. A prompt "as a reply", or words you typed, still bring a reply, under it.

6 · Branches: each keeps its own copy

As ruled: a branch keeps its own copy of your text. An edit or a prompt belongs to the branch it is made in and goes only to that branch's model; no model is sent another branch's changes. The bar's list sends each branch its own, all at once or one at a time, as it does now.

7 · What is stored: shown before building

Your text is a message on your side of the chat, as a prompt is, with one new field saying it is your text and not a prompt. Everything else it holds, a message holds already. The two rulings need no field of their own.

  • writing: optional, true for your own text. A message without it reads as now. Its words go to a model only with a prompt, under the lead line section 4 shows.
  • content and blocks are a message's own fields: blocks is the form a reply keeps once edited in place (C212 (b)), and content its words. Until a model has read your text, both follow what you type; after, content stays as it was read and blocks carries your edits, as for any message.
  • Whether a model has read it is not stored: it has once a model's reply follows it in its chat. An answer from a source does not count.
  • Suggesting in a draft uses the edit records there are, held back (C298). One accepted in a draft is marked gone into your text, with the field an edit has when it goes into a prompt instead of being told (sent: { into, at }, C214 (c)), so it is never told.
  • Branches made by asking several at once copy, with your text, the records of what waits in it, each copy with its own number, as a branch has its own letter. Records of the same kind, in the same stores.
  • Its chat is an ordinary chat. No migration: no record is rewritten, and no store, index or database version changes. A restore checks writing (true, or not there). Backups carry it from this version on; one made before reads as now.
  • An older Parliament restoring a newer backup drops writing, and shows your text as a prompt of yours that had no reply, its marks intact.
  • Drafting Desk's documents, the earlier room for writing your own (R39), are untouched: they stay in their own store, readable in Drafting Desk, hidden as ruled (C288 (a)). Copying one into a room to write in can come with R39, after the refactor.
  • Emptied again, your text's chat stays, empty, until delete:212: nothing you made is removed for you.

8 · Ruled, and to rule

Ruled

  • Branches keep their own copy of your text, each branch's edits and prompts its own.
  • In a draft, Suggesting marks your changes; Editing makes them your text.

To rule

  1. The stored change: one optional field on a message, writing: true, marking it as your own text rather than a prompt, as section 7 shows. Nothing migrated.
  2. Asking several at once: what waits in the text goes to each model asked, each branch then holding its own copy. In every room, replies included.
  3. With nothing typed, answers only in the text: the model is told to write nothing beyond its answers, so a reply of answers alone is one line under your text. In every room.
  4. Ctrl+Enter in your text does what Enter in the bar would, the line under the bar saying what first.
  5. One text per room, its chat's first message; later prompts in the bar go on below it. Writing more of your own further down can come later.
