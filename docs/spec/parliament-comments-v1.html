Comments

C301 (c), proposed. Notes become comments: written on words in the text, on a whole message, or on cards picked in the Collection, and never sent to a model. The comment in the text is as the markup mockup drew it. One change to what is stored is shown in section 5, before anything is built.

1 · In the text

A comment on words: a purple tint over them, then a bubble and its words, small, after the passage. A long one shows its first line and "more". A comment on the whole message, as note:reply writes now, sits under it. Show · Comments in the markup menu hides them all, and its row is no longer faint.

3 · In the Collection

The Notes tab becomes Comments. A comment on words shows them above its own, in its purple; one on picked cards quotes them beneath it, as a note does now. Picked cards are commented on together from the foot, where Note becomes Comment.

5 · What is stored: two fields on a note, shown before building

A comment on words needs to know which words. A note gains two fields, both optional, keeping what a highlight keeps: passage, the words it is on (a highlight keeps them as its text, which in a note is the comment itself), and nth, which time those words come in the message (C285). Nothing else changes.

A note on a reply, now

{
  id: "n-7f3a…",
  num: 233,
  conversationId: "c-201a",
  messageId: "m-91c2…",
  text: "Check the figures against the minutes",
  createdAt: "2026-11-15T16:02:11Z"
}

A comment on words

{
  id: "n-c41e…",
  num: 231,
  conversationId: "c-201a",
  messageId: "m-91c2…",
  text: "Anna wanted five, ask her",
  passage: "the hiring plan",
  nth: 0,
  createdAt: "2026-11-15T16:04:40Z"
}
  • No migration: no note is rewritten, and no store, index or database version changes. A note without the two fields is a comment on its message, or on its own, as now.
  • Backups carry the two fields from this version on. A backup made before reads exactly as now.
  • An older Parliament restoring a newer backup keeps only the fields it knows, so a comment on words comes back as a comment on its message, its words intact. Nothing is lost but where in the message it was.
  • If the words are later edited away, the comment stays on its message, and find it says its words are no longer in the chat, as a highlight's does.
