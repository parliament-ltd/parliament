Where something is missing

Nothing stops working: Parliament does the next best thing and says so in the line under the bar. Popped boards open as ordinary windows, or on this screen. Backups are written uncompressed where the browser cannot compress them. Another tab shows a change when it is reloaded. Outside text is always shown as text only.
